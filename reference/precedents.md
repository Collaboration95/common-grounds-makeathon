# The vibe, and ten useful precedents

Researched 11 October 2026. These are examples to study, not claims that the concepts are unprecedented. Observations below describe the linked work; the suggested applications are our design interpretations. No third-party artwork or audio is copied into the sketches.

The [Common Ground organiser brief](https://luma.com/yjffwqr2) welcomes creative experiments across disciplines and media, including speculative prototypes. Culture includes everyday food, language and communities. My read: a tiny, distinctive experience can fit better than a large utility product. The rubric and presentation length remain unpublished in that source, so “90 seconds” and “five build hours” below are planning assumptions. We cannot predict the judges.

## The two original ideas, examined closely

**Culture idea 1 — Your First Lunch in Singapore.** Its most interesting invention is the relationship between viewer and programme: the fiction turns direct address into an invitation to act. Expand that through one responsive prop, a visible pause while it listens, a satisfying rewind for correction, and a shared-table ending. The original two stalls, multiple orders, lesson recap and host add production weight. The new Living Tape brief explores a single order in a single TV set. Voice is essential to the final premise; typed/simulated inputs in the concept sketch test staging only.

**Culture idea 2 — Kopi Shift.** Its strongest feature is that knowledge becomes a physical choice. The right way to prepare Kopi-O Kosong includes deliberately leaving sugar and milk alone. Expand that through order lettering that is tied to ingredient affordances, a transparent view of the cup's composition, restrained pouring/stirring, and a finished cup that doubles as the explanation. Keep one counter. A second cup can demonstrate the difference without requiring a queue, economy or lesson browser. The new brief explores theatrical feedback while respecting the original rule that already mixed milk cannot simply disappear.

## Cultural grounding

The [Singapore Mandarin Council's kopi-o entry](https://www.languagecouncils.sg/mandarin/en/learning-resources/singaporean-mandarin-database/terms/kopi-o) defines black coffee with sugar and explains the Malay, Hokkien and Teochew elements of the term. This makes the language itself a good cultural material. Food facts must remain authored and reviewed; a charismatic model response is not evidence of a correct recipe. Phrase meanings likewise need context. Specific additional references and open review needs are recorded in each concept brief.

## Principles for this pack

1. One action worth watching from across the room.
2. A first meaningful response within roughly ten seconds of entering the scene; a production target, not a measured result.
3. The cultural detail changes the action, rather than merely decorating it.
4. The prototype has an ending: a served cup, a shared lunch, a phrase performance, an ensemble, a curtain call.
5. A single well-directed 2D or layered scene is enough. Full 3D, camera tracking and runtime generation must earn their cost.
6. Every exciting action has a quiet equivalent and a clear reset.
