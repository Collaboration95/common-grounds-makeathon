Replay code: storyboard.js. Editable export: storyboard.snapshot.json. Portable renderings: storyboard-hero.svg, storyboard-core.svg, storyboard-recovery.svg. Board diagrams abbreviate prior-turn text for space; the table above and preview contain the complete player-facing lines. Input, performance, and next-state production notes sit below every frame.

Accessibility is part of each state: captions do not expire, sound is optional, the slider and Play button replace the drag gesture, visible keyboard focus remains, and still mode preserves the social consequence. A failed voice/camera service cannot occur because none is invoked. A unavailable audio context is caught and leaves visual play working. Narrow screens stack the same scene rather than introduce additional product pages.
