## A host who prompts the player

**User**

- Could we use a Dora-like format?

**Agent**

- Yes. The host can ask the audience a question and pause, while the experience actually listens for an answer.
- For example, a friend asks for rice with roast pork to take away.
- The host asks, “Okay, what do we tell uncle?”
- The player answers, the selected dish lifts, and the food gets packed.
- If the player is stuck, the host gives a hint.
- This keeps the experience playful and small enough to build without needing complex AI behavior.
