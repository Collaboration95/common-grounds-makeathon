# Kopi Shift — 12 states, one counter

Three editable tldraw pages, four frames per page. Frames are states and branches in the same scene. There is no modal onboarding or rush game. All spoken-looking lines are written, explicitly scripted samples in this toy. Sound is optional synthetic feedback.

| 12 · Transfer / final | Cup 01 fresh with “Kopi-O”. “Black coffee with sugar, and no milk, please.” Cup 02 stays served. Meanings may be hidden. | Coffee + sugar; stir; Serve 1. | Same material grammar. Final thank-you returns inside counter. | Complete; Reset counter |

Production rules: O is no milk; Kosong is no added sugar; C uses evaporated milk and sugar. Brewed coffee is a prepared base, not a new recipe ratio. Additions may occur in any order. A final stir is required when more than one category exists; black coffee alone can serve. Meaning and sound toggles preserve state. The clarification and result controls are keyboard buttons. New cup uses an in-place second-action confirmation.

Board shows arrows, ingredients, liquid and absence as editable primitives; notes below each scene supply implementation consequences. See `iteration-log.md` for two critique passes and `board.json` for verification status. `storyboard.js` is repeatable in a tldraw exec context or in the browser where `window.editor` exists.
