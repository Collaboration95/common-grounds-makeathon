# Common Ground Makeathon: Design x Tech x Culture

Source: https://luma.com/yjffwqr2 (rechecked 2026-10-11, including the rendered event date/time), plus the on-site prize slide (see Prizes). Anything not in those sources is marked **UNKNOWN**. Don't invent it.

## What it is
- One-day, in-person creative-tech makeathon in Singapore, **11 Oct**.
- Sits at the intersection of **design, technology, culture**.
- Open-ended: "no fixed medium or outcome".
- Valid outputs: website, visual experiment, AI experience, speculative prototype, or anything else.
- Organisers: Carissa Jayanto, Tanisha Shaw, Kian Seng Ng, with SQ Collective.
- ~121 registered (event was full, waitlist open).

## Tracks (pick one)
- **Design x Tech**
  - "Reimagine how we create, interact and experience."
  - Audience: designers, artists, makers, creative coders pushing tech beyond conventional interfaces.
  - Angle: new interaction models, tools for creation, experiential or sensory work.
- **Culture x Tech**
  - "Reinterpret the past, present and future of culture through technology."
  - Culture = heritage, language, tradition, plus music, fashion, food, subcultures, communities.
  - Angle: preserve, remix, or speculate on culture.

## Teams and eligibility
- Solo, or a team of up to **3**.
- Open to all backgrounds. No design, art, or engineering experience required.

## Tech and tools
- **Codex** (OpenAI) is the primary technology partner.
- Short onboarding session on Codex and other (unnamed) partner tools.
- Optional hardware: Arduino, Raspberry Pi kits, sensors, craft materials, cameras, drawing tablets.
- Bring: laptop, internet access, charger.
