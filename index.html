01 / Culture × Tech / ONE SCREEN

Kopi Shift

Some words you pour. Some words you leave out.

Order typography travels into the cup; Kosong leaves a visible empty sugar space.

Drag a labelled pot, sugar box or evaporated-milk tin into a cup; stir with the spoon. Click-then-cup and keyboard equivalents.

The smallest worthwhile build

One cut-paper counter, two cups, three ingredient categories, Kopi-O Kosong and Kopi-C plus optional Kopi-O transfer; no timer.

Watch for: Negative-space metaphor can blur O versus Kosong; fixed laptop diorama is small on phones; cross-device board sync unverified.

The Tape Can Hear You

An impossible welcome tape. A very ordinary wish: lunch with a friend.

Name a dish and the tray wakes. Correct yourself and the serving props rewind.

Simulated speech keys, R correction, reversible prop slider, S saves the seat.

03 / Culture × Tech / ONE SCREEN

Can Lah!

Two words. A whole world.

Keep Can lah fixed; change the exchange and its typographic, musical, and social world changes.

Drag the words sideways and release to play; keyboard delivery slider and Play are equivalent.

04 / Culture × Tech / ONE SCREEN

Table Chorus

A tissue. Two cups. A little room for someone else.

Clear a shared table: cup rings become eyes and exchanged saucer taps become the smile of a keepsake portrait.

Chope one place, ask, make room, slide a cup closer and take turns tapping a saucer.

The smallest worthwhile build

One top-down table, one device, two people taking turns or one person playing both roles; deterministic SVG keepsake and optional synthetic sound.

Watch for: Chope etiquette varies; the trace-to-face reveal needs clear staging. Human cultural review and listening rehearsal remain outstanding.

05 / Design × Tech / ONE SCREEN

Pocket Stage

A little light. A paper hand. A shadow with a life of its own.

Open the paper hand and its shadow becomes a moth; guide it to a flower that opens in greeting.

Move the light with pointer or arrows; Space opens the hand. Camera tracking is a future optional input.

The smallest worthwhile build

One SVG paper theatre, one original hand/moth silhouette, one flower, reversible controls and an in-scene ending.

Watch for: Inverse light movement may need guidance; shadow geometry is stylised, and camera tracking is not implemented.
